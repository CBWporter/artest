Place the Sutton Hoo Ship in your room

Open the AR view, scan the floor, then position the ship yourself. You can resize and rotate it using normal phone gestures.

How to use the demo

  1. Tap View in your space.
  2. Move your phone slowly to scan the floor.
  3. Tap the floor to place the ship.
  4. Pinch with two fingers to scale the ship.
  5. Use two fingers to rotate it.
  6. Walk around the model to inspect it from different angles.

This demo uses Shiptextured.glb. For iPhone and iPad AR support, add a matching Shiptextured.usdz file and include it with ios-src.
