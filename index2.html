How to use this demo

  1. Open this page on a phone that supports AR.
  2. Tap View in your space.
  3. Move your phone slowly to scan the floor.
  4. Tap to place the ship.
  5. Walk around it, rotate it, or scale it if your device allows.

This page uses Shiptextured.glb. For iPhone/iPad AR, you may also need a matching .usdz file later.
